Add a floppy

Supported: .ST images with 79–84 tracks, 9, 10 or 11 sectors per track and 1 or 2 sides (up to 924 KiB) (e.g. 360, 400, 410, 440, 720, 800 or 880 KiB), and .HFE images (v1, v3; always stored). .MSA is not supported yet.

New disk

An empty, formatted Atari disk: .ST, 720 KiB (80 tracks, 2 sides, 9 sectors). It is added to my floppy images, read-write.

My floppy images

An image that is not Write protected can be written by the Atari (.ST only; new). Written sectors are saved when the drive motor has been off for 2 seconds; wait for “all changes saved” before switching the power off.

The name of RadioFloppy on your network, also used for its WiFi setup network. Letters, digits and ‘-’, up to 32 characters. Takes effect after a restart.
